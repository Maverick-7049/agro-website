Who we are

Bridging innovation and adoption in Indian agriculture.

The Impact Engine's agro practice works at the intersection of agriculture, communication and technology, helping the businesses feeding and equipping rural India get seen, trusted and adopted.

Our story

Built for a category that doesn't communicate like any other.

Agriculture sits at the meeting point of science, policy, weather and trust. A message that works in a boardroom rarely works in a mandi, and a campaign built for a farmer rarely earns a regulator's confidence. We built our agro practice to hold both ends of that conversation, grounded enough for the field, sharp enough for the enterprise.

We work as an extension of our clients' teams: close enough to move fast, independent enough to say what needs saying.
